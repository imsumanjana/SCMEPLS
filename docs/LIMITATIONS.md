# Limitations and required validation

- Default values are illustrative and must not be described as measured results.
- Relative vibration and radar scores do not establish full-scale performance.
- The control-response models are reduced-order and do not include flexible-body modes, actuator saturation, switching delay, sensor quantization, or full six-degree-of-freedom coupling.
- Energy results depend entirely on the entered energy budget and do not estimate magnetic, thermal, or converter losses automatically.
- The SC-MEPLS simulation is a reduced-order model and is not a substitute for Simscape/Multibody, electromagnetic FEA, CFD, structural FEA, or test data.
- The AI calibration module is valid only inside the domain represented by its training data. Cross-validation is not a replacement for external validation.
- Export resolution does not increase scientific validity; 600 dpi only improves publication rendering.
